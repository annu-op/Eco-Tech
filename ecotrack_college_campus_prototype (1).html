Demo mode

Connecting real college data

This file is a local prototype, not connected to your college's meters, ERP, or building-management system. A real deployment can connect through an approved utility-meter API, BMS export, smart-meter gateway, or scheduled CSV from facilities staff.

Before going live: confirm data ownership and access, map each meter to a building, align time zones and units, establish baselines, and restrict personal or sensitive data.

Integration checklist
1. Identify energy, water, and waste data sources.
2. Agree on a standard data schema and update frequency.
3. Validate readings and missing-data handling.
4. Connect an authenticated backend/API.
5. Pilot one building and verify savings before campus-wide rollout.
